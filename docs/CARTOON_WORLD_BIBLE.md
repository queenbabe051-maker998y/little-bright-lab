# The Little Bright Lab — Cartoon World Bible

Audience: children ages 4–10.
Creative promise: joyful, story-led educational animation with expressive animal characters, moving backgrounds, playful reveals, clear English narration, gentle music, and no overlapping dialogue.

## Recurring animal friends
These are the first visual direction for the original cast. Keep the names, colors, and silhouettes consistent across episodes:
- **Mimi** — a cheerful pink bunny; playful, warm, and encouraging.
- **Moomo** — a cream-colored cow with brown patches; lovable, curious, and a little silly.
- **Dott** — a lavender owl; observant, thoughtful, and excited by letters and discoveries.
- **Rumble** — a golden-orange lion cub; energetic, brave, and kind.

These are original character concepts for this channel; final production model sheets can refine details while preserving recognizable silhouettes and palettes. Do not use Pip or a dragon as the host/mascot.

## Five educational series
1. **Animal Sounds Safari** — listen, guess, reveal, repeat; animal sounds, animal names, and habitats.
2. **Little Number Adventures** — counting, number recognition, shapes, patterns, and beginner maths.
3. **Alphabet & Word Explorers** — letter recognition, letter sounds, vocabulary, and early reading.
4. **Bright Smiles Club** — brushing teeth, handwashing, bathing, and everyday healthy habits.
5. **Growing Up Great** — kindness, sharing, friendship, confidence, patience, and naming feelings.

## Animation and audio rules
- Prefer a TV-cartoon rhythm over static slides: character movement, blinking, bouncy reactions, purposeful gestures, scene changes, moving clouds/sparkles, and visual jokes.
- Use a bright, friendly, soft-edged 3D-cartoon-inspired visual language. Keep backgrounds colorful but uncluttered.
- One narrator speaks at a time. Leave a clear pause after questions so children can answer.
- Keep music quiet under narration and avoid sudden loud effects.
- Build each episode around a simple story and a child-participation moment.
- Vertical 9:16 is the default for Shorts; longer episodes can be adapted to 16:9 later.

## Current workflow milestone
The GitHub Actions workflow renders a 60-second animated sampler introducing the cartoon world and previewing the five learning themes. It is a procedural animation prototype, not a finished studio-grade 3D production. The MP4 is produced as an Actions artifact after a successful run.
