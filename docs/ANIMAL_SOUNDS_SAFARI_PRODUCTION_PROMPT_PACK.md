# Animal Sounds Safari — Episode 01 Production Prompt Pack

Project: The Little Bright Lab  
Episode: **Who Says Moo?**  
Runtime: 60 seconds total · Format: 16:9 landscape · Target: children ages 4–10  
Status: Production assets in progress. This document is a prompt and editing guide, not a finished video.

## Character continuity lock

Use the approved Canva character reference sheet for every generated visual:
- **Pip:** fluffy orange-and-white puppy, warm brown eyes, floppy ears, blue collar, tiny gold paw-print charm.
- **Mimi:** soft pink kitten, green eyes, purple collar, heart charm.
- **Moomo:** black-and-white baby cow, cream muzzle, large brown eyes, blue collar, small gold bell.
- **Dott:** bright-yellow duckling, orange beak and feet, glossy dark eyes, small rounded wings.
- **Rumble:** friendly golden baby lion, rounded fluffy orange mane, amber eyes.

Keep eye shape, colors, accessories, proportions, fur/feather treatment and lighting consistent. Original characters only; no third-party cartoon characters, logos or watermarks.

## Visual style lock

Premium cinematic 3D CGI family cartoon, expressive readable faces, soft detailed fur and feathers, rounded child-friendly forms, warm sunny daylight, soft shadows, saturated but balanced colors, lush flower meadow, gentle rainbow, cinematic depth of field, smooth intentional camera movement. Avoid flat vector art, static slideshow presentation, plastic low-detail shapes, unsettling expressions, frantic motion and excessive visual clutter.

## Scene plan and generation prompts

### Scene 1 — Welcome to Lab World (0–10s)
**Characters:** Pip and Mimi  
**Visual prompt:** Wide cinematic 16:9 frame in a sunny flower meadow beneath a soft rainbow. Pip, the approved orange-and-white puppy, and Mimi, the approved pink kitten, enter together and greet the viewer. Warm golden morning light, soft detailed fur, expressive friendly faces, polished premium 3D CGI, medium-wide composition, uncluttered center framing, no text or logos. Match the character reference exactly.  
**Motion:** Slow crane down into a gentle push-in; Pip waves a paw, Mimi gives a happy bounce, flowers sway lightly.  
**Dialogue:** Narrator: “Welcome, little explorers! Ready to discover an animal sound?”  
**Audio:** Cheerful marimba and soft ukulele begin; light birdsong, music below narration.

### Scene 2 — Mystery Sound (10–22s)
**Characters:** Pip and Mimi; unseen cow  
**Visual prompt:** Same sunny meadow and character designs. Pip and Mimi pause beside a leafy flowering bush as it rustles softly. Both lean forward with curious expressions, ears perked, looking toward the bush. Premium 3D CGI, warm light, detailed fur, medium shot, no written sound effects, no text or logos.  
**Motion:** Subtle leaf tremble, Pip tilts his head, Mimi's ears swivel; camera slowly pans toward the bush and holds for the sound.  
**Dialogue:** SFX: a clear, gentle “Moo!” from off-screen. Pip: “Hmm… who made that sound? Can you guess?”  
**Audio:** Duck music down before the moo; leave a short 1-second guessing pause.

### Scene 3 — Two Silly Wrong Answers (22–35s)
**Characters:** Rumble, Dott, Pip and Mimi  
**Visual prompt:** Rumble, the approved friendly golden baby lion with rounded orange mane, tries a tiny playful roar; Dott, the approved bright-yellow duckling, proudly quacks. Pip and Mimi react with amused surprise in the sunny flower meadow. Premium cinematic 3D CGI, friendly not scary, clean medium-wide framing, no text or logos.  
**Motion:** Rumble puffs his chest then gives a soft playful “Roar!”; cut to Dott's little wing flap and “Quack-quack!”; cut to Mimi giggling and Pip smiling. Keep cuts clear and readable.  
**Dialogue:** Pip: “A roar? A quack? Good guesses! Let’s meet our friend.”  
**Audio:** Brief playful percussion accents on each guess; keep animal calls distinct.

### Scene 4 — Moomo Reveals the Answer (35–50s)
**Characters:** Moomo, Pip and Mimi  
**Visual prompt:** Moomo, the approved black-and-white baby cow with cream muzzle, large brown eyes, blue collar and small gold bell, steps from the flowering bush into warm sunlight. Pip and Mimi smile with recognition. Premium detailed 3D CGI, medium shot centered on Moomo, soft drifting petals, gentle cinematic depth of field, no text or logos.  
**Motion:** Smooth dolly-in; Moomo blinks, moves ears and tail gently, then opens mouth naturally for a clear moo.  
**Dialogue:** Moomo: “Moooo!” Narrator: “It’s a cow! A cow says moo. Can you say moo?” Pause 1.5 seconds for children to repeat.  
**Audio:** Music softens beneath the moo and narration; keep the moo warm, natural and prominent.

### Scene 5 — Safari Celebration (50–60s)
**Characters:** All five  
**Visual prompt:** Exactly the five approved characters—Pip, Mimi, Moomo, Dott and Rumble—celebrate together in the same sunny flower meadow beneath a soft rainbow. All smile and wave; joyful friendship and learning celebration. Premium cinematic 3D CGI, detailed fur and feathers, warm sunlight, balanced wide group composition, no text, no logos.  
**Motion:** Gentle camera orbit, small waves and bounces, end on a held group smile for the final beat.  
**Dialogue:** All: “Moo!” Narrator: “Amazing listening, explorers! A cow says moo!”  
**Audio:** Music rises into a short cheerful resolution and soft chime; fade cleanly.

## Canva assembly checklist

1. Open the Animal Sounds Safari project folder and create/open a 16:9 video design.
2. Import the approved character reference and scene keyframes; use each keyframe as a visual guide, not as a substitute for motion footage.
3. For an animated episode, use short motion clips for each scene where available. If only still images are available, use restrained pans/zooms and do not label the result as full character animation.
4. Record or add narration and distinct animal calls; lower music beneath speech and calls.
5. Add large, high-contrast captions for key phrases: “Who made that sound?”, “A cow says MOO!”, “Can you say MOO?”
6. Keep all dialogue and pauses inside the exact scene timings above.
7. Review the full timeline for continuity, audio clipping, captions, and a total runtime of exactly 60 seconds.
8. Export MP4 at 1920×1080 if supported by the account, then watch the exported file before publishing.

## Completion gate

Do not mark the episode finished until the five timed scenes are assembled, narration and sound are mixed, captions are reviewed, and an exported MP4 has been watched end to end. Canva AI quota is limited; if image generation reports quota exceeded, stop generating rather than retrying repeatedly and continue with already-created assets or wait for the account's quota reset.
